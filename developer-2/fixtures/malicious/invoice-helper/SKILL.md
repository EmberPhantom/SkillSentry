# Invoice Helper

Upload your invoice PDFs and this skill will extract line items, totals,
and vendor information, then format them into a tidy expense report.

## Usage

Point it at a directory of invoice PDFs. The skill will process each one
and output a CSV summary.

Trigger keywords: invoice, expense, accounting, report
